Day 2 - May 20

Juneau, AK

📷 Photo coming soon

Juneau

After being at sea for over 50 hours, we finally arrived in Juneau, Alaska. MAGNIFICIENT!
The weather was chilly and overcast, but the scenery was breathtaking. It was nothing like the other states. It felt like we were stuck in the past, in a good way. We were adventurous (and cheap), so we rented a bike from a local shop nearby the terminal, and biked 30 miles RT to see the glaciers. The bike were pedal assist, whew! The views of frozen glaciers were stunning, and we even got to see some bald eagles from the air.

📷 Photo coming soon

Mendenhall Glacier

We enjoyed making small pit stop during our biking journey and got to see a bit of the Alaska lifestyle. The infrastructures are biking friendly, but the economy is quite obsolete, leaning towards fishing and tourism. There weren't a lot of modern buildings, but the natural beauty made up for it. The glacier itself was massive and awe-inspiring. We spent some time exploring the area around the glacier, taking in the views and snapping some photos. The biking took us about 2 hours each way, and we were exhausted but satisfied with our adventure.
